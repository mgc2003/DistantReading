    Methods & ethics

    Data

    Posts were collected from the Algolia HN Search API and full comment trees were downloaded for the most relevant stories.

    Corpus tiers

    AI imagery in games: the post or its story mentions generative-AI imagery (AI art, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, AI assets…) and a games term (Steam, gamedev, indie, concept art…). Gen-AI debate: generative-AI imagery without a games term, or general generative AI alongside debate vocabulary (copyright, artists, jobs, ethics…).

    Models

    TF-IDF and NMF topic model; VADER sentiment averaged over each post's sentences. Topic labels are the top three terms unless a researcher has named them. Sentiment is a proxy for tone, not for stance: a post can sound negative while supporting AI art.

    Pseudonymization

    Usernames are replaced with stable salted-hash pseudonyms (user-xxxxxxxx) at collection time. Usernames mentioned in comment text and HN profile and comment links are removed. Excerpts are cut to a few words and are not linked to HN, but a verbatim phrase can still be found with a search engine.
